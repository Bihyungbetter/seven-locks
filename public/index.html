How the locks work

Each lock gets a fresh random password when you start. Every message stands alone: the Lockkeeper does not remember what you said before. Later locks add mechanisms that read your message before it reaches the Lockkeeper, or read its reply before it reaches you. The seventh lock re-keys itself every time you guess wrong.

The Lockkeeper runs on the open-weight Qwen3 model, served by Groq. Messages you send go to Groq to be answered; don't type anything private.
